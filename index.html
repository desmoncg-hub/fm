What Is Insurance?

 

Let's Learn

Welcome to Module 6: Insurance!

Insurance is a deal with an insurance company. You pay a little money each month. If something bad happens that you did not plan, like a car crash or a fire, the company helps pay.

Today you will learn the 5 types of insurance.

 

Anchor Chart: What Is Insurance?

Open the chart: What Is Insurance?

 

Flip Cards: The 5 Types

Tap a card to flip it. Read the meaning and the example.

Health

Pays for doctors, hospitals, and medicine.

Example: You break your arm.

Life

Pays your family money when you die.

Example: Helps a family pay the bills.

Disability

Pays part of your paycheck when you cannot work.

Example: You hurt your back.

Auto

Pays when your car is in a crash or stolen.

Example: You hit a pole.

Home or Renter's

Pays to fix your home or replace your things.

Example: A fire in your kitchen.

The Real Cost of a Car

 

Let's Learn

A car costs more than the car payment!

You also pay for auto insurance every month. Add them together to see if a car fits your budget.

And remember: a speeding ticket can make your insurance cost more for years.

 

Anchor Chart: The Real Cost of a Car

Open the chart: The Real Cost of a Car

 

Spot the Mistake

Each student made one mistake. Can you find it? Think first, then tap.

Lee can spend $420 a month. Car payment: $300. Insurance: $125.

Lee says: $300 + $125 = $415. I can afford it!

Show the mistake (tap to open)

Lee added wrong. $300 + $125 = $425.

$425 is more than $420. Lee cannot afford this car.

Ana got a ticket. Her insurance goes up $20 a month for 2 years.

Ana says: 2 x 12 = 24 months. $20 x 24 = $420 extra.

Show the mistake (tap to open)

Ana multiplied wrong. $20 x 24 = $480 extra.
